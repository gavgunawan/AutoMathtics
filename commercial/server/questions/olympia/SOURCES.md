# Olympia moons — the sources, and the source check of 20 Sep 2026

Every moon is a set of **original question generators** modelled on one competition's published shape, syllabus and
sample or past papers. Nothing here is a transcribed past-paper question: the papers are the organisers' copyright, and a
generator gives endless variants where a fixed question gives one. What can be checked is (a) that the model rests on the
organiser's own material, (b) that the shape, answer style and topics match it, and (c) that our stored answers are right.
The owner asked for that check on 19 Sep 2026 ("check that the questions are actually from credible sources of previous
paper along with the answers, for all moons"); eight research passes ran on 20 Sep, one a competition, each reading the
sources below, hand-checking 60–130 generated questions and recomputing every answer of 9,000–36,000 generated ones by an
independent script. The faults they found were fixed in v3.85. What they could not fix — every moon sits a notch or two
below the real paper's difficulty, and is text-led where the papers are figure-led — is recorded per moon for the next stage.

## SEA-Moon · SEAMO (Southeast Asian Mathematical Olympiad, Terry Chew Institute / SEAMO official)
- Sources: seamo-official.org/page/guidelines (rules: Papers A–F, 90 min, 20 MC + 5 free response, 3/4/6 marks); the official
  sample papers A, B, C, K with worked solutions and the syllabus PDFs linked from seamo-official.org/resources (answers: yes);
  the full 2018 Papers A, B and C re-hosted by a Vietnamese tuition site (answers: yes, 20 re-derived by hand, all agree).
- Shape: real 20 MC + 5 free response; ours 8 MC + 2 SA keeps the split. Every real paper seen has **five** options with
  "None of the above" (only the sample Paper A shows four) — since v3.85 every paper here has five with "None".
- Fixed 20 Sep: a concert ending at "24:10"; the shortest-path figure (squares, not lattice points); "between" for an
  inclusive range. Arithmetic: 59 of 60 hand-checked right, 9,000 swept clean.
- Still short of the paper: Paper A lacks chicken-and-rabbit, digit counting, repeating-cycle terms, ages, calendars; Paper B
  lacks defined operations, excess-and-deficiency, fencepost intervals, clock-hand angles, catch-up speed; Paper C lacks unit
  digits of sums of powers, telescoping series, paths via checkpoints, draws without replacement, isosceles angle chasing,
  truth/liar logic. Difficulty: A about right for the 3-mark tier, B and C too easy. Real free-response answers include
  decimals, angles and weekdays; ours are integers and fractions.

## US-Moon · AMO (American Mathematics Olympiad, SIMCC with Southern Illinois University)
- Sources: amo.simcc.org (organiser: Grades 2–12, 90 min, CCSSM consulted and extended, syllabus headings 2–4 and 5–6);
  SIU's "AMO InfoPack 2025" (stemedresearch.siu.edu, 56 pp, worked examples per grade with options (A)–(E), answers: yes);
  SIU event pages; the 2024 papers exist on SIMCC's portal behind a login. Any pre-2020 "AMO past paper" online is the
  earlier MOEMS-era 25 × 1-point format, a different paper. amo.sg is dead.
- Shape: real 15 MC (3) + 5 open (5) + 5 open (6) = 25, five options, no calculators, no penalty; ours 6 MC of five + 4 SA
  keeps the 60/40 split. One paper per grade in reality; the 2–4 / 5–6 split is the syllabus heading only.
- Fixed 20 Sep: "21th" ordinals, a 24-hour "13:15" trip time, unreduced "6/9 of the pupils". Arithmetic: 60 hand-checked,
  20,000 swept clean. A seed family no longer repeats within a heat.
- Still short: figure-based counting, Gauss pairing, rate and proportion with a base, fraction of a remainder, ratio with
  transfer, factor counting in words, digit arrangements, multi-letter cryptarithms, elapsed time in am/pm, mean × count,
  unit-cube surface area, decimals and percentages (Grade 5 targeted ideas). Year 2 about right; Years 3–6 too easy.

## SG-Moon · SMC (Singapore Math Challenge, SIMCC)
- Sources: smc.simcc.org (organiser; per-grade syllabus with heuristics skills; calculator allowed from Grade 5); the full
  2023 contest papers, one per grade, at simcc.org/wp-content/uploads (answers: no key published free; the checker worked
  them). The "31 questions: 15 MC + 16 SA" figure in the first research note matched none of the six published papers.
- Shape: real G1–2 40 questions, G3–4 45, G5–6 32, 100 marks, 90 min, almost all short answer written on an answer sheet with
  a few four-option items at Grades 2–4 and none at 5–6; a fraction appears only as an option. Since v3.85 a heat is 2 MC + 8
  SA and fractions are options only.
- Fixed 20 Sep: "3 100-cent coins", ratios not in lowest terms. Arithmetic: 60 hand-checked, 18,000 swept clean.
- Still short: scaled picture graphs feeding a second step, money with change from notes and "k for $p" deals, mass and
  capacity on scales, digit puzzles (broken button, digit counts), figure patterns, three-quantity units problems, spacing,
  ages, logic grids; at 5–6 ratio with change, averages with joiners, discount + GST, rates, tanks with leaks, composite
  angles. Too easy at every band; Sector A's pool is pitched a year above real P1.

## T-Moon · WMI (World Mathematics Invitational, Taiwan)
- Sources: wminv.org (Preliminary Round rules and scoring table; sample questions K–4 and the topic chart); the same
  official sample set with keys at the Hong Kong regional organiser (firsteducation.hk); the retyped 2022 papers (Paper A
  15 MC, Paper B 10 MC) with keys at mathletetraining.com (secondary; 20 keys re-derived, all agree); emosmalaysia.com.
- Shape: real prelim 25 MC of four options — A 15 (10 × 6 + 5 × 8 marks) and B 10 × 10, 40 min each; ours 5 + 5 of four,
  190 s a question, matches in kind. Missing the 8-mark tier and the fraction/decimal answers the real paper has.
- Fixed 20 Sep: the liar puzzle stored the one impossible culprit (now "exactly one tells the truth"); percentages giving
  half children; a negative answer as the only negative option; rupiah decoys ±1; ratios in lowest terms. Arithmetic
  otherwise clean over ~175 checked.
- Still short: order-of-operations openers, symbol equations and cryptarithms, interleaved sequences, position-in-row,
  assumption method, count-the-figures, nets and cube stacks, unit traps, mean/median/mode/range, remainder systems, mixed
  numbers, digit-sum counting. Too easy at every band.

## HK-Moon and BKK-Moon · HKIMO and TIMO (Olympiad Champion Education Centre)
- Sources: hongkongimo.com (five areas, one paper per grade) and its 2019 sample questions with answers; TIMO's official
  sample paper with answers at thaiimo.com (letterhead: Thailand Mathematics Society); the HKIMO Heat Round 2025 papers P2,
  P4, P6 (Indonesia region, EN + ID) re-hosted by a regional organiser (answers: no key; the checker worked them); TIMO's
  heat papers are sold, not published.
- Shape: real 25 short answers, five per category in the order Logical Thinking, Arithmetic, Number Theory, Geometry,
  Combinatorics, 4 marks each, 90 min, no calculators, no penalty; ours two per category in that order at ~210 s ≈ the real
  216 s. Real answers are mostly whole numbers; a few fractions and, at P1–P2, word answers occur.
- Fixed 20 Sep: the four-digit guess had two answers; equal remainders gave a trivial "smallest"; mothers aged 5 or 120;
  "1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … + 1/8"; cycling slower than walking. Arithmetic: 130 hand-checked, 36,000 swept clean.
- The "HKIMO leans on number theory, TIMO on logic" split is **ours**: the official samples are one template with the numbers
  changed and the 2025 papers spread evenly. The headers say so now.
- Still short: cryptarithms, sequences with changing steps, weekday calendars, work-rest timing, give-and-take equalisation,
  working backwards with fractions, custom operations, coin systems, stacked-cube views, pyramids and prisms with n-gon bases,
  min/max area for a perimeter, complement counting, multinomial arrangements, number-theoretic pigeonhole, telescoping sums,
  exterior angles. Grades 1–4 easier than the real paper; 5–6 closest but a notch under.

## PHI-Moon · PhIMO (Philippine International Mathematical Olympiad, Math Olympiads Training League)
- Sources: motlphilippines.wixsite.com/phimo (organiser; per-grade syllabus, five strands); the 2025 heat papers P2, P4, P6
  (Indonesian edition, EN + ID, cover instructions) from the "HEAT PHIMO 2021–2025" folder linked by mathlympia.com; the
  Malaysian licensee's mock excerpts (baxtertrainingconsultancy.com). No answer key anywhere; the checker worked them.
- Shape: real 25 in 90 min, no calculators, no penalty — Part 1 Q1–10 MC of five options with "None of the above" (2 marks),
  Part 2 Q11–25 open (3 then 5); strands mixed, not rotated; nearly every question has a figure. Since v3.85 the five MC
  carry five options with "None"; the one-per-strand rotation is our design.
- Fixed 20 Sep: a tied pictograph marked one child right and the other wrong; a fourth angle over 180°; a negative result;
  unsimplified decimals. Arithmetic: 60 hand-checked, 30,000 swept clean.
- Still short: figure counting and tiling, magic figures and symbol equations, defined operators, remainders/LCM/HCF and
  factor counts, counting ways, meeting dates, time zones, clock rotation, working backwards, work rate, successive
  percentages and mixtures, series sums, pigeonhole, doubling growth, digit reversal. Too easy at every band.

## DC-Moon · AMC 8 (Mathematical Association of America)
- Sources: the AoPS wiki's AMC 8 problems, answer keys and solutions for 2020, 2022 and 2023 (answers: yes; ~35 recomputed,
  all agree); the MAA's 2023–24 AMC 8 Teacher's Manual (rules: 25 MC of five options, 40 min, 1 point each, no penalty, no
  calculators, Grade 8 and below and under 14.5 years; sat abroad through official international partners); Wikipedia.
- Shape: ours ten five-option MC climbing E → M → H matches AoPS's 1–10 / 11–20 / 21–25 bands in miniature. Topic tally over
  three papers: word problems and arithmetic 20 of 75, geometry 14, counting and probability 13, data 9, number theory 9,
  algebra 7.
- Fixed 20 Sep: "start with 5, other digits even" stored 20 (right: 5; 25 for three digits); the routes figure counts
  square to square; the filling kind carried the rates label; fraction decoys in lowest terms. Arithmetic otherwise clean
  over 9,000 generated questions.
- Still short: graph reading in nearly every paper, spatial and nets, pattern extrapolation, custom operations, coordinate
  geometry, ordering logic, constrained optimisation, telescoping products, estimation with units, mode and range. E about
  right; M and H too easy for problems 9–25.

## What the check did not settle
- SMC, PhIMO and the 2025 HKIMO papers publish no answer key; those answers are the checkers' own, worked twice.
- TIMO heat papers are paywalled; its style is inferred from the shared OCEC template.
- No SEAMO paper after 2018 was readable; later drift is unverified.
- maa.org itself sat behind a bot challenge; the manual was read from a mirror.
